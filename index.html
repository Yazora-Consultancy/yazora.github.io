About Yazora Business & Game Consultancy

Through Yazora Business and Game Consultancy, I, Chandan Sahu, bring over 8 years of hands-on experience in mobile game production, PC & Console game development insights, and strategic project management to empower gaming studios and a diverse range of businesses in realizing their creative visions and achieving sustainable growth across all platforms and industries.

We possess a deep understanding of the distinct hurdles within the dynamic gaming landscape, catering to everyone from independent developers crafting innovative mobile gaming apps and indie PC titles to established studios pushing industry boundaries on consoles. Our passion lies in delivering the expert guidance and unwavering support necessary for you to develop exceptional games across the thriving mobile, PC, and Console markets.

Beyond the gaming realm, Yazora extends its strategic project management expertise to support various business entities. We provide crucial business planning, effective team structuring, optimized resource allocation, and well-defined growth strategies for Companies, Firms, Enterprises, Organizations, and Undertakings seeking strategic direction and a solid foundation for success in any industry.
